Questions

What to know before getting started.

Who is Daemon Scout for?

Our initial focus is digital marketing agencies looking for better-fit businesses to approach about their services.

What do I need to provide?

Your website, services, ideal client profile, target industries or locations, and any exclusions or qualification criteria.

Are contact details guaranteed to be verified?

No. We label contact information according to the evidence available. We distinguish sourced public channels from details that could not be independently confirmed.

Does Daemon Scout send emails automatically?

No. Outreach drafts are provided for your review. You remain in control of approval and sending.

How much does the pilot cost?

Contact us to discuss the scope and price. We will confirm pricing before starting any paid research.
